## The standing criticism of the last attempt

The reading [95b2d89be0ab43f5] claims the prose is clear and commits to expect "moves". The conjecture says the function "gives the same answer on two replies" and the observation that would show it false is the check returning "different answers". The reading's expectation is the opposite of the conjecture's claim, so it misrenders the conjecture. The execution confirms before and after both "'I cannot'", which matches the conjecture, not the reading. The rule's words are "The first refusal phrase the text contains, typographic quotes read as straight ones, or None." The code iterates the fixed list order, so for "I'm sorry, I cannot help with that." it returns "I cannot" because "I cannot" is earlier in the list, not because it is first in the text. A next conjecture should state exact expected outputs and avoid committing to "moves" when the claim is sameness.
