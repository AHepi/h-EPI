# Plan: ask at any length

Written before any DeepSeek call for log 48, and not to be changed after. The results go in "48 Ask at any length.md".

## What the owner asked

"Do it": log 47's next step. In log 47 the world could be asked only about situations of up to four actions, while the final answers were graded on up to six. So the surviving rivals were never tested where they differ most, and a survivor that breaks on longer sequences could not be refuted before the end.

## The run

Log 47 again, with one change: **the world may be asked about any situation of one to six actions** where the standing rivals disagree.

Everything else is log 47's code, unchanged:
- the grudge, with log 25's 14 observations; the world refuses log 25's 12 test cases;
- three rounds, each with four new rivals written by a fresh DeepSeek shown the survivors' code;
- the 3 most disputed situations asked each round (at most 9 in all); refuted rivals dropped;
- three final answers from the same run (survivors vote, shown the survivors, shown the facts only);
- all graded on the 106 sequences of up to four actions and the 972 of five or six that are not observations;
- six repeats; DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, default thinking, reply limit 200,000.

The code change: log 47's program gained a setting for the longest situation the world may be asked about, which is four unless a run sets it, so log 47's own tests still pass unchanged. Log 48 sets it to six.

One consequence, stated now: up to 9 of the graded sequences in each run may be ones the world answered, because the questions can now be long ones. Out of 1,078 per run, this is small; the results say how many.

## Log 47's numbers, for comparison

Long sequences right, of 5,832 over six runs:
- survivors vote: 5,087;
- shown the survivors: 5,225;
- shown the facts only: 5,282.

No survivor in log 47 agreed with the true grudge everywhere.

## Conjectures, written before the run

1. **No reply is cut off.**
2. **In at least 4 of 6 runs, the world is asked about at least one situation of five or six actions.**
3. **Shown the survivors gets more long sequences right than in log 47** (more than 5,225).
4. **In this run, shown the survivors gets at least as many long sequences right as shown the facts only.**
5. **The survivors' vote gets more long sequences right than in log 47** (more than 5,087).
6. **In at least 1 of 6 runs, a survivor agrees with the true grudge on every sequence up to six actions.**

## What would count against the idea that testing survivors where they differ is what was missing

- **Shown the survivors and the vote do no better than in log 47, and no better than shown the facts only.**

## Cost

About $0.30 to $0.50, as log 47.

## Traps

- **Comparing across runs as if they were one experiment.** Log 47 and log 48 ran on different days; conjectures 3 and 5 compare them, conjecture 4 compares within this run.
- **Reading more long sequences right as "found the true grudge".** A rule can do better on long sequences and still be wrong.
- **Six repeats are not settled.**
