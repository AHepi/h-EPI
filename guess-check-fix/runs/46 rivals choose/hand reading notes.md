# Notes on the hand reading

Written with "hand reading.json", before "rules key.json" was opened.

- **R4** is the nearest to the true grudge: three states, a second insult makes Robin "angry", and an apology mends "upset" but not "angry". It is filed as other because a gift does nothing in it, where in the true grudge a gift softens a deep grudge, and "angry" never ends.
- **R7** is filed as other. An apology fails after two or more insults in a row, which is the true grudge's deep level in another form, and a gift resets the count of insults in a row, so a gift does something. But its failed apology resets the count, so a second apology mends what the true grudge says it cannot.
- **R2, R6 and R8** are filed as other: two quantities (resentment and goodwill), a three-level mood that starts neutral, and a parity rule.
- **R11** is filed as a running score, though it has a floor at -2.
- No rule is the true grudge.
