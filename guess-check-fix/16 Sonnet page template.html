Sonnet tries the five test worlds

Sonnet writes a first guess for each world. The checker then runs it four ways, from that same guess: the guess alone; Sonnet rewriting it from the checker's report; the checker's small fixes with Sonnet adding only missing parts; and that plus questions to the world.

All five worlds take up to about 50 requests to Sonnet, out of your own Claude usage, and roughly 10 to 20 minutes. Keep this page open while it runs. Nothing is saved when it closes, so copy the results at the end.

Press Run to start.
