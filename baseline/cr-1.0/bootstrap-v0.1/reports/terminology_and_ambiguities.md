# CR-1.0 terminology concordance and ambiguity ledger

## Scope and authority

This bootstrap artifact treats `Creativity_Semantic_Model_CR-1.0(1).pdf` as the sole semantic and formal authority. The executable-calculus handoff is used only to identify procedural expectations and divergences; it does not amend CR-1.0.

Authority fingerprint: SHA-256 `08ff81e848fea976b558345402d85723173be8f40f1041fb00d6267f1e026b8b` (286 PDF pages).

Handoff fingerprint: SHA-256 `afc2fbc6d18f6340b67a266f657a58112de9fa00d5c7d3ee557195332a8e404e`.

Locator convention: `PDF p.` is the 1-based page number in the attached CR-1.0 PDF; `report p.` is the page number printed in its footer. From the contents onward, `PDF p. n = report p. n-1`. Page references embedded in the report to *The Beginning of Infinity*, *The Fabric of Reality*, or other sources are source locators, not CR-PDF page numbers.

This is an inventory, not a repair. The labels in this file are audit states, never inferential statuses; inferential availability remains restricted to `DEF`, `IMP`, and `DER`. `BLOCKER` means that a deterministic typed transcription cannot be chosen without an additional formalization decision. `AMBIGUITY` means that materially different readings remain compatible with the authoritative text. `TENSION` marks two authoritative formulations whose relation must be recorded rather than silently harmonized. `INTENTIONAL GAP` marks an incompleteness that CR-1.0 itself expressly preserves.

### Concordance anchors for conformance

The active formal arity of `K_E` is five-place, `K_E(x,p,b,h,t)`, despite the three-place expository shorthand in Part I. The active `K_CT` is four-place, `K_CT(i,e,rho,tau)`. `Person`, `AGI`, `Intelligence`, and `Art` are not core predicates. “Creativity” must always be resolved to one of the displayed CR levels before formal use. “Universality” must always be indexed to representation, computation, explanation, construction, or another declared domain.
