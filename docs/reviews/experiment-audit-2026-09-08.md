# An audit of the experiments, 8 September 2026, and what the harness took from it

**Status.** An external audit of this repository at commit `d80a60c` (8 September 2026), supplied by the maintainer as a PDF whose text is reproduced below. It is kept here, as the earlier reviews are, because its subject is the gap between what the records carry and what the write-ups say. The section after the text says which of its findings the harness agrees with, what was built in reply and where each change is recorded, and which of its proposals were not taken and why. Nothing in this file is evidence about any model; the evidence is in the records the register entries cite.

## The audit as received

The text below is the audit's, extracted from the PDF and unchanged except for line breaks and the hyphenation the extraction left behind.

>
> Audit of the h-EPI experiments
> Branch audited:claude/harness-repeatability-and-grounding-options of AHepi/h-EPI, head commit
> d80a60c (2026-09-08). Three independent audit passes were run: an inventory pass over the full tree, a
> methodology pass over all eight experiment write-ups, and a code/reliability pass over the harness source
> and the failure-modes register.
> What exists
> The repo is a fail-closed, criticism-first LLM conformance harness: models fill JSON forms from docu-
> ments under numbered instructions, every observation and run is a content-addressed immutable record,
> conjectures are pre-registered universal statements in claims.jsoncommitted before the runs that test
> them, every comparison family is read against an in-run REPEAT noise floor, replay re-scores recorded
> replies offline, and an appraisal layer tracks contested keys. There are 9 pilots and 15 run directories
> totalling 11,051 observation records and 82 run records: the travel-claim battery in three rounds plus
> an 18-model sweep (4,110 records, ~$10), a cycles family testing self-revision against the repeat floor
> on five models (405 records plus an 81-record 600-second rerun), a reasoning-level factor experiment
> on the two gpt-oss models (162 records), three semantics-battery pilots (labelling 780, distinctions 620,
> derivations 860 records plus an 860-record replay re-score and an 860-record repaired v2 rerun), and the
> unit-dependence and unit-controls families on the semantics document (1,821 and 468 records). The
> failure-modes register documents 35 harness defects, nearly all fixed with guarding tests, which is gen-
> uinely unusual practice.
> Principal findings
> The machinery is methodologically stronger than typical LLM evaluation work — pre-registration, mea-
> sured noise floors, replay, liveness witnesses, an exemplary error-correction culture (S18 fully withdrawn
> and reversed after the renderer defect H28 was found). The problems are in inference, not record-keeping.
> The largest standing threat to validity is that the repeat floor is measured but never subtracted in the
> machine. Comparison verdicts are byte-exact, and causation is attributed only in prose; the endpoint
> itself is non-stationary (gpt-oss:20b’s floor was 1/18 in the morning and 6/18 the same afternoon on
> byte-identical requests), so every two-call comparison inherits an unmodelled confound. Second, there
> is no inferential statistics anywhere: headline cross-model claims rest on n=9–20 with effects the same
> order as the floor, and roughly 150 “never”-form conjectures are refuted almost mechanically by floor
> noise, inflating the 49-refuted headline count. Third, endpoint confounds are pervasive: shared lanes,
> RemoteDisconnected-versus-timeout ambiguity (the timeout is per-socket-operation, not end-to-end), no
> per-call wall-clock field, and missing data handled by prose rather than machine (gpt-oss:20b@high lost 20
> of 27 calls yet conclusions were still drawn; the stopped boolean run left 296 orphan records). Fourth, the
> tooling has exploitable gaps: no dedup between original and replayed runs in claims evaluation (double-
> count risk, currently avoided only procedurally), refusal detection consulted only after JSON recovery
> succeeds, and machine tables that disagree with prose corrections in at least four places (XPRT-01, CYC-
> 14, DER-07/14/20, CYC counts) — only the prose is trustworthy. Fifth, several headline claims rest on
> contested ground: the distinctions battery’s strongest finding has 25 of 45 refutations contested on one
> reading; LEN-01 (800-token replies never missed an unambiguous multiplied total) is near-tautological
> and arguably scope-tuned, and the small controlled factor experiment pushes against the sweep-level
> “reasoning implies correct arithmetic” narrative; “five models of five sizes” is really a reasoning-versus-
> not contrast, since the two gpt-oss models span nearly the same active-parameter range.
> Generated by Kimi
> Creative solutions
> The unifying principle: every caveat currently carried in prose should be promoted to a typed, content-
> addressed record with a fail-closed consumer. The no-ranking philosophy survives because every statistic
> outputs bounded classifications, never scalar model scores.
> Priority zero (validity of any current or future claim). Emit ATTRIBUTION records pairing each
> comparison delta with its simultaneously measured floor, yielding a ternary ABOVE_FLOOR /
> WITHIN_FLOOR / INDETERMINATE verdict. Replace “never” conjectures with pre-registered
> rate conjectures carrying confidence bounds (Clopper–Pearson lower bound versus floor upper bound),
> and use e-values for anytime-valid inference so stopped runs yield valid inference records instead of
> orphans. Add per-call wall-clock and lane-provenance fields, with records lacking them excluded from
> causal families by machine. Split transport errors into an enumerated taxonomy and add an end-to-end
> deadline alongside per-op timeouts. Introduce typed SUPERSEDES/REPLAYS edges between runs
> with enforced dedup in claims evaluation. Schedule experiments as interleaved crossovers (A,B,A,B
> across the day, order randomized per lane) so endpoint drift enters all arms equally and becomes an
> estimable block effect; pair manipulated calls adjacently in time and analyze within-pair discordance
> with an exact McNemar-style test, which is tiny-n friendly and refutation-consistent. Classify every
> refutation as SUBSTANTIVE, FLOOR_ARTIFACT, VACUOUS, or KEY_UNDERDETERMINED,
> and enforce table/prose consistency by making prose cite record hashes with a checker that refuses
> publication when a CORRECTS edge exists and is not acknowledged.
> Priority one (before the next cross-model write-up). Refusal-first detection recorded even when JSON
> recovery later succeeds. Pre-registered minimum-yield rules that auto-emit UNDERPOWERED records
> (gpt-oss:20b@high would have been excluded by machine). Negative-control families (manipulate content
> that cannot matter; pre-registered expectation is floor-level effect) and placebo-key families to anchor the
> hallucination floor. Holdout conjecture validation: tune thresholds on a calibration set, then evaluate
> a sealed hashed validation set, which directly defuses the LEN-01 scope-tuning critique. Key plurality:
> pre-register multiple stipulated keys and emit KEY_SENSITIVITY records; if an effect flips sign across
> plausible keys the verdict is KEY_UNDERDETERMINED, which is itself a publishable instrument
> result.
> Priority two (deepens the science). Drift sentinel probes — fixed byte-identical prompts interleaved in
> every schedule as a real-time stationarity meter feeding the noise model. Adversarial key generation,
> where a model other than the one under test proposes rival keys that enter human appraisal, making key
> appraisal adversarial rather than author-stipulated. Reasoning effort as a randomized within-model factor
> (2×2: capability × effort) instead of the confounded size ladder. Repair LEN-01 as an explicit mediation
> chain — length to machine-detectable reasoning trace to arithmetic correctness — with a verbose-placebo
> arm (long padded replies with no reasoning) that must show no correctness gain. Refutation survival
> reporting: a refutation counts only after surviving a replay re-score on a different day and lane; ones that
> vanish get FRAGILE status, quantifying how much of the register is endpoint weather.
> Generated by Kimi
## Where the harness agrees

The diagnosis is accepted on these points, and each is now a fact the machine records or refuses rather than a caveat in prose.

- **The repeat floor was measured but never applied by the machine.** True until this change: a `never` claim was refuted by one observation and the floor sat in another table. Each refutation is now placed against the REPEAT observations of its own run and case (`all`, `some`, `none`, `absent`), the counts are in `refuting_by_floor`, and every committed claims file was re-evaluated: across 381 claim evaluations, 175 refuted, on 6,680 refuting observations, 5,328 are `all`, 210 `some`, 672 `none`, 470 `absent`. Register entry H39; `docs/how-it-works.md`, "Conjectures, tested by the records"; `docs/what-the-records-refute.md`, "Each refutation against the repeat floor". The audit's estimate that "roughly 150 never-form conjectures are refuted almost mechanically by floor noise" can now be read against the records: the refutations whose condition held on the refuting observation and on no other repeat of the same case are the `none` class, 672 observations, and most of those belong to conditions a plain repeat cannot satisfy at all (a cycle's verdict, a removal's relation, a failure on the longest calls), where the class is structural. The audit's number was an estimate; the class is a count, and neither is a verdict.
- **No record said how long a call took, and a timeout could not be told from a disconnection.** True for every record before version 3. Each attempt now carries `started_at` and `elapsed_ms`, and a failed attempt carries `transport_kind`, read from the exception exactly as the `transport_error` claim predicate reads it. Register entry H37; the first records that carry them are the two runs in `forge/conformance/runs/travel-claim-v3-order/`. The audit's remark that the timeout is per socket operation, not end to end, is correct and was already the substance of H34; the elapsed time now makes the difference visible in the record.
- **Refusal detection ran only after JSON recovery had failed.** True. A configured refusal phrase is now recorded wherever it occurs, and beside a recovered object it raises `REFUSAL_SUSPECTED` with plural loci. Register entry H36. No live record yet carries the flag, and that absence is recorded.
- **A reply and its replay could be counted twice.** True, and avoided by hand. A replayed observation now names its source in `replayed_from`, and `claims` refuses a set that holds both, or one observation twice. Register entry H38. Records written before version 3 carry no source; the derivations pair (`signed-derivations` and its re-score `signed-derivations-visible-key`) is such a case and the documents that cite them say which they read.
- **Prose should cite record hashes and a checker should hold it to them.** Taken as written. `python tools/check.py cite`, part of `all` and of the offline suite, resolves every sixteen-hex record id cited in `docs/`, `README.md`, `agent.md` and `CLAUDE.md` to exactly one record file in this tree or one path in `docs/archived-records.txt`, which lists the incident-form records that live on the archive branch. Of 646 citations at the time of writing, 586 name a record in this tree, 58 one on the archive branch, and two (H22) are declared by their own sentence as a re-score that was not committed; the checker lists those and fails on anything else.
- **Order should be randomised so that endpoint drift enters every arm.** Taken in part. `--order shuffled --seed N` sends the cases in an order drawn from the seed, each case's variants still together, and the run record carries `order` and `shuffle_seed`. Interleaving across the day (A, B, A, B) is a scheduling decision for the person running the battery, and the record now says what order was used; the harness does not schedule.
- **Machine tables and prose corrections disagreed in places.** True where the register says so: XPRT-01's refutation rests on a re-score that was not committed (H22); the derivations claims were read from the re-score, not the original (H28); the cycles counts were corrected in `docs/cycles.md` (H34). The citation checker holds every id; it cannot hold a sentence to a table. The two remaining disagreements are named here and in the register rather than resolved silently: XPRT-01 stays unrefuted in the travel-claim table until the re-score is committed in its own directory, which `claims` can now refuse beside its source; the derivations tables cite the record set they were read from.

## What was not taken, and why

- **Rate conjectures with Clopper–Pearson bounds, e-values, and an exact McNemar test.** Not taken. Each is a statement about a sampling model: identical requests to one endpoint on one day are not a sample from anything a bound describes, and the audit's own finding (the floor of gpt-oss:20b moving from 1 in 18 to 6 in 18 within a day on byte-identical requests) is the reason. A lower bound printed beside a model's name would be read as a warrant, and the constitution forbids warrant from counts. What is kept is the count and the class, with the observation ids, so that a reader who wants a bound can compute one and own the assumptions.
- **An ATTRIBUTION record with an `ABOVE_FLOOR` / `WITHIN_FLOOR` / `INDETERMINATE` verdict.** Taken as a description and not as a verdict. `all` on a change-against-baseline condition is what the audit calls within floor; `none` is what it calls above floor; but "above floor" attributes the change to the manipulation, and two calls cannot carry that attribution when the endpoint is non-stationary, which the audit also says. The class names what the records show and stops there.
- **Classifying every refutation as `SUBSTANTIVE`, `FLOOR_ARTIFACT`, `VACUOUS`, or `KEY_UNDERDETERMINED`.** Three of the four exist under other names and the fourth is a promotion. `FLOOR_ARTIFACT` is the floor class read with the condition; `VACUOUS` is the liveness witness (H21: a condition that held nowhere has not been shown able to fail); `KEY_UNDERDETERMINED` is the appraisal's `contested` and `defeated` standing (H18, H24). `SUBSTANTIVE` would say a refutation is real, which is the one thing the machine never says.
- **`UNDERPOWERED` records from a pre-registered minimum yield.** Not taken as a label. The run label `INCONCLUSIVE_NO_SCORED_OUTPUT` already marks any run with an unscored field or a failed call, the record says how many calls returned nothing, and a threshold would be a number the author chose. The audit's example is right on the facts: the reasoning-level run of gpt-oss:20b at `high` lost twenty of twenty-seven calls (R5, H34), `docs/reasoning-levels.md` says so with the record ids, and every statement about that level rests on the seven replies that arrived. Whether seven is enough is the reader's decision, not the machine's.
- **Holdout conjecture validation.** Not needed as a separate mechanism: pre-registration is the holdout. A conjecture is committed with the pilot change before the records exist, and one written afterwards is marked as a description of them. LEN-01's timing is a matter of record: it was committed at 07:50Z on 6 September, after rounds one to three and before the sweep's records, and `docs/what-the-records-refute.md` says so.
- **Key plurality with `KEY_SENSITIVITY` records.** Partly present. A declared ambiguity names a field and each rival reading gives the field's key under the rival rule; the appraisal records which readings have been argued about and with what standing. A separate record kind for the sign of an effect across keys was not built; the appraisal's `contested` is where a key-dependent refutation already lands.
- **Negative controls and placebo keys.** Partly present. The unit-controls pilot is a set of such controls (no document, the claim's own argument, a renamed vocabulary, a negated claim), and a control whose corruption leaves the reference output unchanged is refused when the plan is built (H20). A placebo key, a key that is wrong by construction, was not added; it is a pilot, not a code change, and can be written when a battery calls for it.
- **Drift sentinel probes.** Not built. A byte-identical probe sent in every run is a case appended to a pilot, and the REPEAT family already measures within-run drift; a cross-run sentinel needs a fixed case in every pilot and a table over runs, which `compare` half provides. Open.
- **Adversarial key generation by another model.** Refused on the constitution: the appraisal's arguments are written by a person and never extracted by a model (`docs/reviews/README.md`). A model's proposed rival reading could be an input a person considers, but it does not enter the appraisal by machine.
- **A capability by effort factorial.** Not run. Only the gpt-oss models take a reasoning level, and those models are no longer being called on the maintainer's instruction; the reasoning-level runs that exist are recorded with their limits in `docs/reasoning-levels.md`.
- **LEN-01 as a mediation chain with a verbose placebo arm.** Not built; a good design, and it is a pilot's work: a padded-reply control is a variant family with a manipulated instruction, not a change to the machine. Open.
- **Refutation survival across a day and a lane, with a `FRAGILE` status.** Not taken as a status. A second run on another day is a new run of new calls, and `compare` already sets two runs of one model beside each other request by request; a refutation that appears in one and not the other is a fact `compare` reports, not a status the claim carries.
- **An end-to-end deadline beside the per-operation timeout.** Not added. The elapsed time is now recorded, so a call that ran long is visible in its record; a second deadline would be a second way to lose a reply.
- **"Only the prose is trustworthy."** Disagreed as a rule. Prose and tables are both readings of the records, and where they differed the register says which was corrected and by what (H22, H28, H34). The records are what is trustworthy, which is why the citation checker holds the prose to them.
- **The stopped run's 296 orphan observations.** Not deleted and not given a record. They are complete observations without a run record, listed as such in `docs/document-dependence.md`; a run record written after the fact would describe them, and the harness does not write descriptions.

## Changes to the machine made in reply, in one place

Record version 3 (observation and run), with the version 2 schemas kept frozen as `conformance-observation.v2.schema.json` and `conformance-run.v2.schema.json` and every version 2 record loading and replaying its id unchanged (`tests/test_record_v3.py`); per-attempt `started_at`, `elapsed_ms`, `transport_kind`; `refusal_phrase_present` beside a recovered object; `replayed_from`; `order` and `shuffle_seed` with `--order shuffled --seed N`; `claims` floor classes and its refusal of a reply beside its replay or of one observation twice (`tests/test_claim_floor.py`); `tools/check.py cite` with `docs/archived-records.txt` (`tests/test_check_cite.py`). Register entries H36 to H39.
