# SMF-0.1 underspecification atlas

## Status and boundary

This is a non-authoritative diagnostic of the CR-1.0 semantic model class. It is not a source anchor, accepted interpretation, bridge declaration, theorem, or amendment to CR-1.0. The sole authority for the existing CR-EIB bridge remains the 286-page PDF with SHA-256 `08ff81e848fea976b558345402d85723173be8f40f1041fb00d6267f1e026b8b`.

The atlas distinguishes five claim origins: `SOURCE_AUTHORITY` for what CR-1.0 explicitly states; `EXTERNAL_SOURCE_REPORT` for bounded reports about other primary or engineering sources; `SOURCE_INTERPRETATION` for a proposed reading of source material; `PROJECT_IMPORT` for an operator-supplied conjecture or modelling addition; and `FORMAL_CONSEQUENCE` for a result derived from stated formal material. External reports cannot enter the candidate model without an explicit interpretation or project-import bridge. No row is accepted merely by appearing here.

The central criticism candidate is not that CR-1.0 has too few named relations. It is that several relations which carry the intended meaning remain substantive primitives whose admissible interpretations may be only partly constrained. The weak calibration fixture shows label-floating only in that deliberately reduced projection. Whether two incompatible role assignments can satisfy a dependency-closed CR theory over the same target-neutral history is the open `SMF-CR-CM-01` obligation, not an established result.

## Target-neutral comparison language

Before testing labels such as `Problem`, `AttemptExp`, `Criticism`, `Authors`, `Explains`, or `K_E`, the forge needs a deliberately weaker comparison language. Its provisional observational vocabulary contains typed events; partial-order histories; tokens and transformations; declared system boundaries; provenance edges; intervention families with held-fixed conditions; content identity/equivalence claims; causal accessibility; persistence/reconstructibility; and resource bounds. This vocabulary is an engineering comparison surface, not yet a theory of meaning.

For a fixed target-neutral base, the decisive question is whether two incompatible assignments of epistemic roles can both expand it to a CR model. If so, the encoded constraints do not implicitly determine those roles. A second, independent check existentially erases newly added vocabulary. If the repaired theory then reduces to the old theory over the base vocabulary, it demonstrates no old-language model restriction. It could still narrow rival role expansions over each preserved base history, so calling it merely notation additionally requires failure on that separate axis.

## Operator-supplied episode conjectures

The operator has supplied four refinements after CR-1.0: attention to a contradiction initiates an episode only within recognition and values; conjecture precedes criticism; a response may suspend the issue unresolved or move on, where moving on includes acceptance or rejection; and episodes may overlap, interact, or be absorbed. These are live `PROJECT_IMPORT` conjectures until their relation to the authority and any successor semantic model is explicitly adjudicated.

They generate immediate tests. An unattended mismatch and an attended problem must remain distinguishable without equating attention with conscious report. Suspension must differ from rejection and from mere inactivity. Ignored criticism must differ from criticism that was never available. Two overlapping episodes must not be forced into one total sequence, while cross-episode causal influence must remain representable. Any formalization that makes criticism logically or temporally prior to its object fails the stated role ordering.

## Non-inductivist research and test invariants

The forge must reject any transition from repeated instances, survival, citation count, model agreement, benchmark accuracy, posterior probability, or absence of found criticism to truth-warrant. Statistical machinery may propose or order conjectures and searches. It may not establish their content, semantic authority, or probability of truth.

A research question is itself a conjectured criticism opportunity. It is useful only when different possible answers discriminate live rivals or attack a shared premise. A search that finds nothing does not support the queried claim; protocol v1 leaves that question open because it cannot prove exhaustive execution. A failed empirical test criticizes the tested conjunction, including auxiliaries and interpretation, rather than mechanically isolating one theory. Passing a challenge records only `UNREFUTED_BY_THIS_CHALLENGE`.

## Initial prioritized frontier

Here, the earlier reference to protocol v1 is historical. Active v2 preserves the same nonconfirmation rule: an unsuccessful search leaves its selected question live and supplies no support. Its plural triage also keeps candidate, auxiliary, test, and scope criticisms jointly live until future evidence-bound human workflow treatment; scheduling one does not isolate it as the cause.

The first frontier is implicit determination of the core semantic roles. The forge should seek twin expansions of the same target-neutral history in which `Problem`, `Attempt`, `Criticism`, `UsesReason`, `Authors`, or `K_E` receive incompatible assignments. This directly tests whether the current model does more than name its intended meanings.

The second frontier is the problem–conjecture–criticism episode. It should construct minimal pairs for problem recognition, conjecture priority, reason-specific criticism, ignored or suspended criticism, endpoint/problem-situation separation, and overlapping episodes. The operator-supplied refinements remain separately typed while source research asks which are recoveries, extensions, or corrections.

The third frontier is the semantic ratchet. Every proposed repair must name the bad model it removes, preserve protected positive witnesses and prior exclusions, retain fixed scope and modality, expose new imports, and provide an independence witness showing that deleting the repair restores a concrete defect. A trade-off is a semantic revision requiring criticism and possible research, not a numerical improvement.

The fourth frontier is research reconstruction. CR-1.0's source registry and citations are a seed map, not a substitute for the lost reasoning history. Primary passages should be recovered falsifier-first: explicit denials and boundaries, changes across Popper's works, cases that cross a proposed distinction, strongest scholarly rival readings, and hidden auxiliary assumptions. The result should be a claim–source–argument graph whose open forks may motivate proposed research warrants only after the failure-locus and route gates are discharged.

Mathematical extraction begins only after a distinction has identity conditions, positive and negative witnesses, an open falsifier, and resistance to relabelling, existential erasure, and hostile minimal pairs. Until then, formal satisfiability is evidence about an encoding—not evidence that the intended semantic class has been captured.
