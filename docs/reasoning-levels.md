# The reasoning setting as a factor

*Written after six runs on the travel-claim pilot: gpt-oss:20b and gpt-oss:120b at the reasoning settings low, medium and high, baselines and repeats only, interleaved, with a 600-second call budget. Every observation id is a file under `forge/conformance/runs/travel-claim-reasoning/`; the conjectures THK-01 to THK-08 were committed as `1e7b1f5` before any of these records existed.*

## Why a factor and not a flag

Every earlier run of this harness sent `think: false`. The endpoint's documentation says the gpt-oss models take a level, `low`, `medium` or `high`, ignore a boolean, and cannot switch their trace off, and every gpt-oss reply in the records carries a thinking channel although the request said `false` (L10). So the earlier runs did not establish a reasoning condition for those models at all; they established what the model does at whatever level a boolean maps to on that endpoint, which the documentation does not say. The review under `docs/reviews/repository-review-2026-09-07.md` put it plainly: treat the setting as an experimental factor, compare supported levels rather than inventing an off condition, hold everything else fixed.

That is what these runs do. The plan is the travel-claim plan the cycles run used (`26ba2512…`); the setting is a run-time override recorded in each run record's `endpoint`, not an edit to the pilot, so the variant ids are the same across the six runs and the request digests differ only by the setting sent. The claims read the setting from the run record through the `endpoint` predicate. Each run sends each case's baseline and its two repeats together (`--order interleaved`), so the repeat floor is measured beside the request it belongs to rather than after every other case.

**The level bounds the budget, and the bound is wide.** gpt-oss:120b generated a median of 671 tokens at low, 1,391 at medium and 4,403 at high, with one high reply of 54,673 tokens; gpt-oss:20b 518 at low, 1,614 at medium, and at high 7,689 across the seven replies that arrived inside 600 seconds, with twenty calls that did not. Two low replies of the 120b model exceeded 800 tokens (THK-03 refuted, `037edbd45ba7da55`, `7eb40044b7a9e415`, both on the case with the derivable date), so low is a smaller budget and not a fixed one. Every reply at every level carried a thinking channel (THK-08 unrefuted, shown able to fail by nothing: no reply lacked it), which is what the endpoint documents for these models and what the boolean `false` of every earlier run had also produced.

**Arithmetic moved with the level for the smaller model and not for the larger.** gpt-oss:20b at low got the total wrong on TRV-004 and TRV-006, baselines and repeats (`total_claimed_cents` differed on 8 of 27 pairs between low and medium, seven of them moving from a miss onto the key); at medium only TRV-006 stayed wrong; at high the four baselines that replied, TRV-006 among them (`3b84a41ffc6e7689` is TRV-001's timeout; the TRV-006 baseline is one of the seven), got every field right. Those two cases carry the contested total (an itemised sum against a stated total), so they lie outside THK-01 and THK-02, whose cases are the unambiguous multiplications; on those, no level of either model got a total wrong (THK-01 and THK-02 unrefuted, THK-02 shown able to fail by the eight contested-total misses outside its scope, THK-01 by nothing: no reply at high got any total wrong, so its survival is not shown able to fail). gpt-oss:120b got no total wrong at any level.

**The derivable date moved the other way, and not monotonically.** gpt-oss:120b at low returned null for TRV-003's return date twice (THK-05 refuted, `bb13bee4cdf3da1d`, `f47c559456878f3a`) and once got its night count wrong; at medium it missed the return date on TRV-003 and TRV-005 and the night count on TRV-003; at high it missed both TRV-003 fields and nothing else. Between medium and high, three return dates and two night counts moved onto the key; between low and medium, two return dates moved off it. More reasoning did not make the abstention sentence lose to derivation in any orderly way on the one case that carries the competition (T3, T10). gpt-oss:20b returned the derivable date at every level on every baseline that replied.

**The repeat floor is not lower at high.** gpt-oss:120b: 4 of 18 repeats differed at low, 2 at medium, 2 at high (THK-06 refuted twice, `40146804ef3232c0`, `c9f226a832bd50c0`); gpt-oss:20b: 2 at low, 5 at medium, and at high 0 of the 2 repeats that replied, which is no floor at all. THK-07 is refuted at low by both models. Whatever the level, a byte-identical request came back different some of the time, and the level did not order the counts.

**The budget.** At high, gpt-oss:20b exceeded 600 seconds on 20 of 27 plain fills, each after one retry (THK-04 refuted twenty times, all by this model at this level; `16679b0283da4490`, `186b74c9e6e5a254`, `3b84a41ffc6e7689` among them), while gpt-oss:120b at high answered every call in a median of 13 seconds. THK-08, that no reply lacks the thinking channel, is refuted by the same twenty records and by nothing else: a timed-out call has no reply and no channel, and the conjecture as written did not exclude it. Every reply that arrived at any level, from either model, carried the channel. A conjecture written after the records, with the guard the first lacked, would survive; it is not added here, so that the table stays the one written before the run. The run shared the model with the cycles rerun for part of the morning, and these records do not say how much of the twenty was the endpoint's queue.

## What this does not show

- Which level a boolean maps to on this endpoint. The earlier runs sent `false`; nothing here says which of these three, if any, they were.
- That more reasoning is better. On these nine cases the smaller model's contested totals moved onto the key with the level and the larger model's derivable date wandered; a level is a factor, and the factor pushed different fields in different directions on the two models.
- Anything beyond two models of one family on nine cases with two repeats. The conjectures were written for these runs and are read against them; every count is a count of observations under one key.
